What this wiki is about

This lens tracks which industrial segments AI / cloud data-center expansion actually propagates through.

Within the unified research system, it serves as the chain entry point for lens-based analysis, not a memo about who is moving the most today:

  • First, confirm whether chain position is real;
  • then check whether monetization distance is near or far;
  • only then ask whether the elasticity-adjusted price signal is worth trading.

Why this chain deserves its own wiki

  • IEA estimates global data-center electricity use was about 415 TWh in 2024 and could reach close to 945 TWh in the 2030 baseline. The U.S. and China together contribute nearly 80% of the growth.
    Source: https://www.iea.org/reports/energy-and-ai/energy-demand-from-ai
  • DOE / LBNL estimates U.S. data-center electricity use was about 176 TWh in 2023 and could rise to 325–580 TWh by 2028, increasing share of total U.S. electricity from about 4.4% to 6.7%–12%.
    Source: https://www.energy.gov/articles/doe-releases-new-report-evaluating-increase-electricity-demand-data-centers
