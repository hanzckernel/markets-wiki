Position of this page in the unified Equity wiki

This page is not for a single chain or one theme cluster; it is the methods page for the full unified research system. It merges the old chain-research workflow of “confirm chain position, then industry calibration, then compress the judgment” with the cross-theme watchlist workflow of “bucket first, then compare by a unified scoring discipline.”

Shared discipline

  1. First confirm which chain or bucket a company/page belongs to, rather than chasing short-term price moves.
  2. Prioritize primary disclosures, official product pages, annual reports/quarterly filings, and official press releases.
  3. Industry-level theses only calibrate; they do not replace company-level evidence.
  4. Final outputs should be compressed into reusable judgments, not narrative stacks.

Source layers

  • Frozen TradingView watchlist / normalized manifest: internal watchlist snapshot layer
  • Raw market snapshots: internal market snapshot layer
  • Official IR / annual report / quarterly results / product pages: private primary-document layer
  • Chain-level calibration: IEA, DOE/LBNL, Berkeley Lab, technical media, and others

Extra rules for chain-research lens

  • Confirm chain position first, then define bull case / risk / one-line judgment.
  • Ask three questions:
    • Who truly resolves the bottlenecks of data-center expansion;
    • Who is just being mapped into the theme;
    • Who has such a long monetization chain that extra risk discount is needed.
  • Classification thresholds:
    • direct: product/service is itself the core bottleneck in data-center expansion.
    • conditional: logic is valid, but monetization strength depends on projects, cadence, or end-user structure.
    • edge: theme-related but mostly mapping, pilot, or non-core revenue.

Extra rules for cross-theme watchlist lens

Five scoring dimensions

  • quality_moat
  • growth_driver
  • valuation_setup
  • sentiment_setup
  • risk_control

Industry valuation framework

  • Platform tech / semis: prioritize forward PE, and use P/S if needed.
  • Financial institutions / capital markets: check earnings quality and regulatory sensitivity first, then PE.
  • Fintech / consumer credit: prioritize P/S, earnings pathway, and source of capital.
  • Pharma / biotech: combine PE, pipeline, patent cliffs, and policy risk.

Action mapping

  • Buy: top attraction on the current risk/reward setup in this bucket.
  • Hold: still worth monitoring, but current odds, risks, or valuation are not strong enough for top-tier status.
  • Sell: weakest setup in this bucket, or a story too far ahead of execution.

As-of and maintenance discipline

  • Comparison pages refresh the internal market snapshot layer first, then update actions and total scores.
  • Raw captures are immutable.
  • Every durable update must keep index.md and log.md in sync.
  • Temporary watchlist names should not be treated as durable structure.
  • Canvas is for navigation, not exhaustive listing; Excalidraw historical material is no longer active layer.
