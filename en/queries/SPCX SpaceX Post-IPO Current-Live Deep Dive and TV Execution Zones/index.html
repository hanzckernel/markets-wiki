    0. Current conclusion — 2026-09-08 action-level review¶

    The Cursor acquisition closed on 2026-08-14. It must no longer be described as an option or merely potential dilution. At the 2026-09-04 regular close of USD147.95, the conclusion is: the action-level re-underwrite is complete. Revoke every pre-Cursor USD85-180 numeric execution zone; no price-triggered buy/add permission is active. Only future post-Cursor disclosure can change this conclusion.     This is not a mechanical rule that every acquisition voids every level. The Q2 10-Q and closing 8-K change both the per-share denominator and the cash-flow bridge. Basic shares were 13.181780bn on 2026-07-28. Closing issued 391.042m basic shares, producing 13.572822bn, or +2.967% from that base. Adding the disclosed assumed 29.128m RSUs and approximately 44.365m options gives a 13.646315bn gross-conversion scenario. It is neither GAAP diluted shares nor a guaranteed floor: it assumes those awards convert, excludes pre-existing/unreported awards, and ignores option exercise proceeds. A future GAAP diluted count may be above or below this sensitivity.

    The Q2 10-Q reports USD100.009bn cash plus marketable securities and USD39.364bn debt plus finance leases, or about USD60.645bn net cash. But six-month operating cash flow of USD3.466bn less USD28.476bn capex is conventional CFO-minus-capex of -USD25.010bn. At USD147.95 and the gross-conversion-scenario denominator, equity value is about USD2.019T and net-cash-adjusted EV is about USD1.958T. This is a pro forma date-mixed snapshot: 2026-06-30 cash/debt paired with the 2026-09-04 price, not a current cash-balance claim. The old USD85-180 map used 13.076bn pre-Cursor shares, treated Cursor as optional, and used stale rough cash/debt inputs; it no longer has execution authority.

    A USD50bn / USD60bn Cursor equity-value premise is only about USD3.664 / USD4.397 per gross-conversion-scenario SPCX share. It is not an intrinsic-value target and cannot substitute for post-close Cursor revenue, margin, capex, or exact fully diluted-share disclosure. No replacement numeric band is created.
