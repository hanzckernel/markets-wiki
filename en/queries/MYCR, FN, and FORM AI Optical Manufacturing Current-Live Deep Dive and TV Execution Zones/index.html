    MYCR, FN, and FORM: AI Optical Manufacturing Current-Live Deep Dive and TV Execution Zones¶

    Original view Deep-research verdict More accurate formulation

    Fabrinet is the best risk-adjusted US-listed name Supported narrowly; not proven across the whole market Within the covered US-listed complex-optical-manufacturing / recognized-optical-revenue scope, FN has the strongest volume-production proof. That cannot be extrapolated from two non-homogeneous US stocks to the entire US optics universe.
    FormFactor is the most direct test/yield name Supported It is one of the most direct listed expressions of wafer probe / KGD / test interfaces; it is neither an HBM producer nor a CPO pure play.

    The ranking must be separated by dimension:

    • Research payoff: MYCR > FORM > FN.
    • Current quality / risk-adjusted broad rubric: MYCR ≈ FORM in the same tier; FN one tier lower. A subjective 0.03-point gap should not manufacture a precise winner.
    • Recognized optical-manufacturing revenue proof: FN #1.
    • Test / yield directness: FORM #1.
    • Optical-assembly hidden-exposure payoff: MYCR #1.
    • Large-position confirmation: NO for all three. Only observation positions / small research positions in stages are supported.

    The original order cannot be preserved as the single ranking across all dimensions. It did identify three real volume-production bottlenecks, and MYCR remains research priority #1.

    Why they rank near the top¶

    1. MYCR — die placement / bonding: monetizes sub-micron placement, flip-chip, eutectic/epoxy bonding, and wafer-level processing for III-V lasers/detectors/modulators, silicon photonics, and carriers.
    2. FN — coupling / packaging / integration / volume production: monetizes precision alignment, optical/electronic packaging, production test, NPI-to-volume execution, and supply-chain execution.
    3. FORM — wafer probe / KGD / opto-electrical test: screens bad die before expensive HBM/chiplet packaging and brings optical + electrical testing to silicon photonics / CPO.

    The common evidence ladder is:

    capability → customer qualification → order/backlog → shipment → recognized revenue → margin → OCF/FCF

    • MYCR has reached capability + aggregate demand, but optics-only economics remain open.
    • FN has reached recognized optical/DCI revenue + volume production, while FCF still needs to catch up with expansion.
    • FORM has reached directional HBM/CPO demand + company-level financial proof, while standalone HBM/CPO economics remain missing.

    These are complementary process nodes, not homogeneous peers. A single node cannot be extrapolated into the sole winner across the entire CPO chain.

    Company verdicts¶

    MYCR: hidden exposure, not a hidden bargain¶

    • MRSI-LEAP targets <±1 µm at 3σ, >1,000 UPH, and 24/7 operation, with flip-chip, UV epoxy/curing, and wafer-level packaging support. Its silicon-photonics page describes 0.5 µm placement and III-V-to-silicon eutectic processing.
    • Vanguard/PWB can relax pre-positioning tolerances at specific optical interfaces. It cannot replace die attach, all fibre coupling, thermal/electrical interconnects, reliability, or testing. The issuer still says customers are mainly research and innovation centres.
    • H1 orders +61%, sales +17%, and EBIT margin 33.2% are real. Global Technologies / High Volume are aggregates, Pattern Generators contributed about 80% of H1 divisional EBIT, and the customized SLX was explicitly a one-off.
    • Normalization uses about SEK 9,250M of sales, a 23.85% EBIT margin, and roughly SEK 8.78 of EPS; it does not double the H1 33.2% margin.
    • Verdict: research priority #1; true hidden-value gem = NO. See MYCR — Mycronic.

    FN: data center moved from inference to the largest reported end market¶

    • Q4 FY2026 revenue was USD 1.3158B, up 45%; non-GAAP EPS was USD 4.10. Both exceeded the old guidance high. Q1 FY2027 revenue guidance is USD 1.375B–1.425B, with a midpoint about 43% higher year over year and 6.4% higher sequentially.
    • The new end-market disclosure makes the old datacom/telecom debate more direct: data center was USD 669M, up 68% year over year and 13% sequentially, or 51% of total revenue; communications infrastructure was USD 413M, or 31%.
    • A secondary call summary quotes management saying DCI annualized run rate exceeded USD 1B; new transceiver programs could ramp as early as the current quarter, and a merchant program was expected to start in the December quarter. NPO/CPO remains program/opportunity proof, not standalone revenue.
    • According to the call summary, the four FY2026 customers at or above 10% were Cisco 20%, Nvidia 16%, Nokia 11%, and Amazon 11%, totaling 58%. This is more diversified than the old two-customer narrative, but aggregate concentration remains high.
    • The cash gate remains open: Q4 FCF was USD -36.9M, FY2026 FCF only USD 4.2M, and Q4 non-GAAP GM 12.2%. Building 10 and other expansion can amplify growth but also utilization/ROIC risk.
    • At 2026-08-18 05:11 ET, Nasdaq early premarket was USD 541, down 9.62% from the 598.58 prior close, with bid/ask 540.02 / 544.00 and still-thin volume. It was only about 5.12% below the 8/14 close of 570.22.
    • USD 541 implies roughly 31.4–32.9x a third-party FY2027 EPS range and is near the old USD 536.55 SMA200. This is an expectations/valuation reset, not an operating-thesis break or regular-session sell confirmation.
    • Verdict: HOLD / no forced trim / no fresh chase. Large-position confirmation remains NO. Whether an overweight position should be partially sold depends only on the portfolio-weight gate; the thin premarket decline is not a sell trigger. See FN Fabrinet.

    FORM: test/yield directness is real; price discipline is tighter¶

    • The HBM path is wafer probe / KGD / test interface. The CPO path sits mainly in Systems opto-electrical testing.
    • The December 2025 acquisition was Keystone Photonics. The 2026-07-28 announcement with Keystone Microtech/KSMT expanded a partnership; it was not an acquisition.
    • Q2 revenue was USD 258.2M, up 31.9%; GAAP/non-GAAP GM was 50.7%/53.3%; Q2 FCF was USD 52.6M. The issuer proved only that HBM and CPO drove sequential growth in Probe Cards and Systems respectively.
    • USD 83.45 was the 7/29 close before the earnings release. The next close was USD 105.38, and 8/4 was USD 120.30. This was a positive post-Q2 reaction, not a collapse.
    • Standard YTD return was +115.67%, with mechanical TTM GAAP P/E around 81x. Q3 guidance containing about a 300bp one-off GM benefit cannot be multiplied by four as the action-band denominator.
    • Verdict: most-direct test/yield = YES; large position = NO. See FORM — FormFactor.
