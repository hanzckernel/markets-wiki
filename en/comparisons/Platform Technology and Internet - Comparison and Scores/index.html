    Platform Technology and Internet - Comparison and Scores¶

    Last updated: 2026-04-16 17:36 CEST Parent page: Platform Technology and Internet Method page: Methods and Sources Scoring input: 2026-04-16 Market Snapshot（源文件保留在私有研究库）

    How to compare this group¶

    • Compare platform control, distribution power, and AI monetization pathways first, then valuation multiples; one megacap being more expensive does not automatically push it to the bottom.
    • Valuation is based mainly on a mix of forward PE + P/S, avoiding single-multiple comparisons between AMZN’s capital-heavy platform and AAPL’s mature hardware platform.
    • TSLA — Tesla remains in this basket, but as a conditional name it is first given a risk discount.

    Quadrant overview¶

    • X-axis = Advantage strength = (quality_moat + growth_driver) / 2
    • Y-axis = Cost controlability = (valuation_setup + risk_control) / 2
    • The farther to the upper right, the stronger the pros and the more controlled the costs in this group.

    Current ranking¶

    1. MSFT — Buy — Enterprise distribution control and the Azure AI monetization path are the clearest; despite a premium, it remains the highest-conviction core slot in the platform basket.
    2. AMZN — Buy — The AWS + ads + retail triple-engine is still working, and on a platform-basket basis this valuation is not the most constraining.
    3. GOOG — Buy — Search cash flow provides enough cushion, and Cloud AI continues to improve the growth structure, making value-for-cash balance comparatively reasonable.
    4. META — Buy — The ad engine and AI efficiency gains are both paying off, but high CapEx and regulatory pressure keep it slightly below the most stable platform spot.
    5. AAPL — Hold — Quality is exceptional, but it currently acts more like a mature platform defensive holding; the further acceleration of AI-driven device replacement still needs confirmation.
    6. TSLA — Sell — Long-dated optionality is large, but the mismatch between auto cash flow and very high valuation is still the hardest-to-manage risk in this group.

    Layered conclusion¶

    Buy¶

    • MSFT: Enterprise distribution control and Azure AI monetization pathway are the clearest; despite a premium, it remains a high-conviction core slot in the platform basket.
    • AMZN: The AWS + ads + retail triple-engine is still working, and on a platform-basket basis this valuation is not the most constraining.
    • GOOG: Search cash flow provides enough cushion, and Cloud AI continues to improve the growth structure, making value-for-cash balance reasonably balanced.
    • META: The ad machine and AI efficiency gains are both materializing, but high CapEx and regulatory pressure keep it slightly behind the most stable platform position.

    Hold¶

    • AAPL: Quality is exceptional, but currently it is more like a mature platform defense position; AI-driven acceleration of replacement cycles still needs confirmation.

    Sell¶

    • TSLA: Long-dated optionality is large, but mismatch between car cash flow and extreme valuation is still the hardest risk to sustain.
