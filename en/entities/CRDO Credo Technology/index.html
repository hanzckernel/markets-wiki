    CRDO — Credo Technology¶

    Updated: 2026-04-14 23:03 CEST Industry page: Interconnect, Optics, and Power Components Classification: Direct

    Chain position¶

    AEC, retimer, high-speed interconnect chips, and optical/electrical DSP platform.

    Why it is in this chain¶

    It addresses a real high-speed interconnect issue in AI scale-out networking fabrics.

    Current bullish logic I am willing to acknowledge¶

    AEC + DSP + optical module roadmaps are clear; this is currently one of the most recognized interconnect chain leaders.

    Main risk I care about now¶

    Customer concentration is high, and hyperscaler capex timing can amplify volatility.

    One-line conclusion¶

    【Interconnect】AEC/retimer positioning is real and is one of the leading current names. Customer concentration is high.
