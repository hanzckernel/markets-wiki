    GSK — GSK¶

    Updated: 2026-04-16 02:45 CEST Industry page: Pharma and Biotechnology Classification: direct

    Theme position¶

    Vaccines, specialty medicines, HIV, and multi-therapeutic pipeline.

    Why it is in this theme¶

    GSK is now a purer biopharma asset, with vaccine cash flow and an increasing focus on growth in specialty medicines and next-line pipeline.

    My current preferred bullish case¶

    As specialty medicines rise in mix and there are enough late-stage assets, if all 14 priority innovation opportunities materialize, GSK’s structural improvement is not yet fully reflected.

    My current key risks¶

    Vaccine volatility, launch pace of key products, and legacy litigation can all affect valuation; a slower pipeline conversion would stall re-rating.

    One-line view¶

    【Pharma】Vaccine and specialty-medicine structure is improving; re-rating still has room if pipeline conversion continues.
